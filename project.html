👞 How should Chelsea boots fit when new?

Chelsea boots should feel snug around the instep and heel with slight room in the toe box. Since genuine leather stretches 5-8% after 2-3 wears, never buy a loose pair!

🌧️ How to protect riding & leather boots during Gujarat monsoons?

Apply beeswax or mink oil balm once a month. If boots get drenched, stuff them with dry newspapers and air dry away from direct heat. Visit our Bardoli shop for monsoon water-repellent treatments!
